Cala
by Cala
Trip to Another Hackathon
−
The internet is full of data. Most of it is messy, scattered, unreliable.

At Cala, we turn messy, scattered, unreliable data into structured, exhaustive, verified data. That's our bread and butter, and it's why we're building the knowledge layer AI agents rely on.

But we're also obsessed with something else: craft. Taking the time to build something good and lasting. The kind of care that goes into a handmade object, a well-composed photograph, a poem that says more with less. Data doesn't have to live in a spreadsheet. It can be art.

So here's the challenge: pick a topic you're curious about, use Cala to dig into it, and find the most compelling way to show what you found. An infographic, an interactive piece, a game, a short film, a poem, whatever the story needs.
Good data deserves to be seen, not just queried.

A few sites that inspired this challenge:
https://neal.fun/size-of-life/
https://neal.fun/wiki-spy/similar/49905/
https://ordinaryabundance.com/

How we'll judge it:

Creativity & originality - is this an angle or format we haven't seen before?
Storytelling & clarity - does the piece actually communicate something?
Execution & craft - is it well-made, polished, and does it feel finished rather than rushed?
Use of Cala - how deeply and thoughtfully did Cala’s data shape the final piece?
